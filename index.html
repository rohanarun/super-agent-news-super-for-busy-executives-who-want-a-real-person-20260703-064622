How Super compares in the personal assistant landscape

Siri

Voice‑first and deeply embedded in Apple devices. Great for simple commands, limited for multi‑step computer workflows.

World‑class conversational AI and planning assistant. Powerful for thinking and drafting, but repeated computer work doesn’t automatically get cheaper or faster.

Gemini

Google is aggressively pushing computer use inside Gemini, signalling where the market is heading [memeburn.com].

Grok

Opinionated assistant with real‑time and social context. Useful for awareness, not designed as a durable executive operator.

Folk

Niche tools within the broader automation and agent market. Often focused on specific workflows rather than full executive delegation.

Experimental approaches to automation and agents. Early explorations rather than a hardened executive assistant.
